Financial structures built around business and project needs.

Start with what your business or project needs. BWCI’s Financial Engine is being developed to connect that need with a suitable financial structure, capital sources and delivery partners.

Start with the economic need.

01 · The starting point

Begin with the real constraint.

A business, place, or sector has a need that existing financial products do not serve well. That need — not whatever product happens to be available — sets the direction.

+

Starting from the need avoids forcing a deal into the wrong financial structure.

BWCI’s intended role is to design and coordinate structures at holding-company level. Regulated activity would be carried out by appropriately authorised operating entities or independent partners.

The Financial Engine

Six steps, one loop: from economic need to productive activity, and back into reinvestment.

Reinvestment returns to need

A real business or project need is identified and understood: its cash flows, contracts and constraints.

Illustrative exampleA regional food manufacturer wins a large export order. It needs working capital to buy inputs now, but the buyer pays in 120 days.

A structure is designed around the need, with terms, timing and repayment matched to how the activity earns.

Illustrative exampleThe facility is sized to the order and repaid from the buyer’s payment, rather than through a standard term loan.

The structure is matched to the platform, group company or authorised partner able to deliver it.

Illustrative exampleDelivery sits with the entity that holds the relevant capability and, where needed, the regulatory permission.

Capital whose mandate fits the structure, risk and tenor is connected through the appropriate authorised vehicle.

Illustrative exampleCapital providers looking for short-tenor, trade-linked exposure are matched to the facility.

Capital reaches the underlying activity and is put to productive use.

Illustrative exampleThe manufacturer buys its inputs, fulfils the order and keeps its workforce and suppliers busy.

Performance is tracked, returns flow back, and what was learned informs the next structure.

Illustrative exampleThe buyer pays, the facility is repaid, and the engagement informs how the next order is structured.
