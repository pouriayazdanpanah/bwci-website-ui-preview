01 · The starting point

Begin with the real constraint.

A business, place, or sector has a need that existing financial products do not serve well. That need — not whatever product happens to be available — sets the direction.

+

Starting from the need avoids forcing a deal into the wrong financial structure.

BWCI’s intended role is to design and coordinate structures at holding-company level. Regulated activity would be carried out by appropriately authorised operating entities or independent partners.
