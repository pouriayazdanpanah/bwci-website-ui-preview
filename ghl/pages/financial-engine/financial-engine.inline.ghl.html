Start with the economic need.

01 · The starting point

Begin with the real constraint.

A business, place, or sector has a need that existing financial products do not serve well. That need — not whatever product happens to be available — sets the direction.

+

Starting from the need avoids forcing a deal into the wrong financial structure.

BWCI’s intended role is to design and coordinate structures at holding-company level. Regulated activity would be carried out by appropriately authorised operating entities or independent partners.

Stage 01 / 07 Identify Need
STAGE 01 · DIAGNOSTIC

Identify Need

What Happens

We assess what the business or project needs: its commercial reality, trade cycle, asset life and working-capital pattern, and why standard bank finance has not filled the gap.

WHO

Our financial engineering and structuring team, supported by market and industry insight.

Where Execution Occurs

Assessment and feasibility work happen at holding-company level. Regulated client onboarding and statutory checks are conducted through authorized operating entities.

STAGE 02 · STRUCTURING

Design the Struture

What Happens

We design the financial structure: debt or equity, tenor, risk layers (senior, mezzanine, junior), collateral and covenants, the cash-flow waterfall, and how assets are ring-fenced to protect investors.

WHO

Capital architecture and governance specialists, supported by legal and regulatory advisors.

Where Execution Occurs

Designed at holding-company level with input from institutional risk committees. Legal ring-fencing is put in place before the structure is assigned to a vehicle.

STAGE 04 · MOBILISATION

Mobilise Capital

What Happens

We bring in money from institutions like pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, private debt funds, family offices, and development finance institutions (DFIs). Each institution has its own risk and return requirements.

WHO

Our capital architecture team, supported by institutional investor networks and Providence Asset Management Ltd

Where Execution Occurs

Through institutional syndication and regulated fund vehicles, with capital ring-fenced for each facility

STAGE 05 · EXECUTION

Deploy capital

What Happens

Capital goes to work: funding trade invoices, financing equipment, or paying for construction milestones. Money is released as agreed. Operational milestones are verified.

WHO

Authorized operating subsidiaries and regulated partner banks, using controlled disbursement systems.

Where Execution Occurs

Disbursements flow only through authorized entities, licensed partners, or special-purpose vehicles (SPVs), into the operating accounts of real businesses.

STAGE 06 · Monitoring

Learn

What Happens

We track how each structure performs: cash flows, how quickly invoices are paid, collateral values, and debt service cover. This data tests whether the original assumptions were right.

WHO

Our technology and infrastructure capability, supported by performance reporting, collateral management agents (CMAs) and independent audit.

Where Execution Occurs

Platform data feeds back into holding-level governance, risk management, and lending criteria.

STAGE 07 · EXPANSION

Scale

What Happens

Structures that work are standardized and rolled out to similar sectors, supply chains, and regions, becoming repeatable programs.

WHO

Every team across the Financial Engine, working together.

Where Execution Occurs

Through platform growth, and, where needed, licenses in new jurisdictions.
