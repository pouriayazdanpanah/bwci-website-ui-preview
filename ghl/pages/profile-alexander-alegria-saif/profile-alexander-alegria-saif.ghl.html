Biography

Alex Alegria-Seif is Chairman of Better World Capital Investments PLC, the UK holding company of the group’s FCA-regulated asset management business. He has more than 25 years of experience in cross-border investment, corporate finance and the circular economy. He has built and governed businesses in 11 countries across Latin America, Europe, the Middle East, Asia and Africa.

His work rests on a conviction that took shape after a personal turning point in 2011: lasting change starts with the people who lead it. In his words, change the doer, not just the doing. Applied to the economy, this means small and mid-sized businesses should not have to stand alone.

Alex founded an international metals business that generated hundreds of millions in revenue. He then founded Metaluck in Dubai to integrate the circular zinc value chain across Latin America, Africa and Asia. As Group President of Ithaka Holding, Dubai (DIFC), he invests long-term capital in financial infrastructure (asset management, trade finance and banking) and in a portfolio of industrial-regeneration businesses.

He is an alumnus of Harvard Business School’s Program for Leadership Development and holds a Master of Engineering Science from the University of New South Wales, Sydney.

Alongside his commercial work, Alex serves as a director of the Restate Foundation in Switzerland and the World Impact Forum in Dubai. He supported the first systems-change investment guide, co-published by CSP and MIT.

His current focus is bringing institutional governance and patient capital to collectives of circular-economy SMEs and regenerative businesses. The goal is a paradigm shift from the current economic model to one centered on collective wellbeing, in which the businesses that create real-world value also share in owning the system that finances them
