How value circulates

Capital, capability and commerce move through the network together, and each flow is designed to reinforce the others.

01 · Capital flows

Wholesale capital, matched to real needs

The model is designed to bring wholesale and institutional capital to verified business needs, through appropriately authorised vehicles.

What we intend to measure

Capital deployed into productive business activity.

02 · Capability flows

Shared institutional infrastructure

Independent platforms can share institutional-grade underwriting, compliance and digital infrastructure, so each one does not have to build its own.

What we intend to measure

Reduction in duplicated operating overhead across participants.

03 · Commerce flows

Connected supply chains

Businesses in the network can find, trade and work with each other. That builds commercial relationships which may strengthen resilience across regional supply chains.

What we intend to measure

Trade volume between participants and continuity of regional supply chains.

Three ways to access capital and capability

How growing businesses typically get capital and capability, and where the Better World model differs.

Model A

Standalone

  • Often faces higher borrowing costs
  • Experiences little or no access to wholesale capital
  • Remains more exposed to trade and market shocks
  • Achieves limited commercial reach
Typical outcomeConstrained growth
Model B

Consolidation

  • Founders typically give up ownership and control
  • Decisions move to a central corporate structure
  • Priorities may shift towards the acquirer's returns
  • Culture and foundational identity can be lost
Typical outcomeAbsorbed
Model C

The Better World Ecosystem

  • Retains complete independence without requiring a sale or merger
  • Accesses capital structures designed around specific needs, where appropriate
  • Leverages shared institutional capability and infrastructure
  • Connects to a growing network of aligned businesses and partners
Intended outcomeIndependent, productive growth

Governance, technology and risk

These safeguards apply across the whole ecosystem, not just to individual participants.

01

Group governance

Board oversight, capital-allocation frameworks and ethical standards set at group level.

02

Risk & compliance

Regulated activities sit with appropriately authorised entities. Counterparties are screened before any engagement.

03

Secure digital infrastructure

Institutional-grade interfaces with strict data segregation, using tokenised structures only where appropriate.*

04

Transparent reporting

Reporting built to be verified. We will publish outcomes as the ecosystem matures and state clearly what exists today and what is still in development.

*Technology, including tokenisation, is used only where it serves the need. It is never a default answer.
