Institutional pathways into the real economy

A structured connection to the real economy

BWCI is developing the architecture and holding layer of the Better World ecosystem. Its role is to identify real-economy needs, design the right structures, and connect the capital and capabilities to meet them.

Where institutional capital could connect

Depending on the opportunity, structure, mandate, and regulatory requirements, capital may be deployed at different points in the architecture.

These are potential areas of participation, not current investment opportunities.

How opportunities are structured

The structure determines the route for capital. That route may involve a BWCI platform, a Group company, an independent financial institution, or a qualified partner, depending on the opportunity and the regulatory requirements.
