Governance framework

Five layers of oversight

01

Board of Directors

The Board sets strategy and is accountable for the company’s conduct and capital allocation.

Meet the Board →

02

Board oversight & committees

Committees support the Board in oversight of audit, risk and appointments.

03

Capital-allocation governance

Capital allocation decisions follow defined approval thresholds and are recorded and reviewed by the Board.

04

Risk, compliance & conflicts

Risks, compliance obligations and conflicts of interest are identified, recorded and managed under Board-approved policies.

05

Regulated vs non-regulated activity

Better World Capital Investments PLC is the holding company. Regulated activities are carried out only by operating entities and partners holding the relevant authorisations.
