The problem

There is no shortage of capital. The challenge is getting it to productive businesses in the right shape. There is no shortage of capital. The challenge is getting it to productive businesses in the right shape.

The product comes first

Too often a business is asked to fit the finance, when the finance should be shaped around the business.

The pieces arrive separately

A company that needs working capital, equity and trade finance usually has to find, negotiate and fit them together on its own.

The terms don't fit the work

Short-term money for a long-term asset, or rigid repayments for a seasonal harvest, can put real strain on an otherwise sound business. For investors, that mismatch is avoidable risk.

The Financial Engine

Six steps, one loop: from economic need to productive activity, and back into reinvestment.

Reinvestment returns to need

We start with what an organization or project needs to grow, operate, or invest before considering any financial product.

We then design the financial structure around that need: the form, terms, and capital ownership that fit the activity.

Next, we identify the institution or delivery capability best placed to provide that system, within the platform or through a partner.

Capital is then matched to the structure, from sources whose terms and time horizon suit the activity.

The capital is put to work in productive activity: the business operates, trades, employs, and builds.

Performance is reviewed, lessons are recorded, and capital and insight are reinvested to meet the next need.

Better World

What “Better World” means in practice

For us, a better world is not a label added at the end. It starts with practical questions asked at the beginning: who will this capital serve, how long does it need to stay, and what will it help to build or restore? For us, a better world is not a label added at the end. It starts with practical questions asked at the beginning: who will this capital serve, how long does it need to stay, and what will it help to build or restore?

“Impact should influence the architecture of capital itself.”

So we bring impact in early, when capital is structured, owned and deployed. The result is investment designed around long-term productive and regenerative outcomes, not reported on afterwards.

The approach is created to support four outcome areas.

01

Productive enterprise

Capital that reaches SMEs and entrepreneurs, and the employment they create.

02

Resilient trade and supply chains

Finance structured around how goods and services actually move, so working capital supports trade instead of holding it back.

03

Regenerative business and sustainable infrastructure

Patient, long-term capital for productive assets, and for businesses that restore rather than deplete.

04

Stronger regional economies

Capital that circulates in the communities where it is earned, supporting local resilience together with, where relevant, financial inclusion.

We will only report outcomes we can evidence. Our Impact and Outcomes methodology, setting out what we will measure and how, is in development and will be published here.
