Reconnecting capital with the real economy.

Mission

Better World Capital Investments PLC exists to improve how capital reaches productive economic activity. We develop and coordinate financial architecture that connects the needs of businesses and projects with appropriate capital, institutions, technology and strategic partners.

Our objective is to support productive growth, stronger and more resilient businesses, and measurable long-term economic impact by designing financial structures around the underlying economic need rather than starting with a predetermined financial product.

BWCI’s role

Better World Capital Investments PLC is the holding company and financial architecture layer of the Better World ecosystem.

Its role is to:

  1. identify and understand underlying economic and financing requirements;
  2. design appropriate financial architecture around those requirements;
  3. connect businesses and projects with relevant sources of capital, institutions and capabilities;
  4. coordinate participation between investors, financial institutions, operating entities and strategic partners;
  5. develop, own and support financial platforms and investment vehicles within the Better World ecosystem where appropriate;
  6. facilitate the integration of capital, financial capability, technology and strategic relationships; and
  7. support the development of long-term financial infrastructure serving productive economic activity.

Who does what

BWCI architects, connects, coordinates and develops the financial ecosystem; the appropriate operating or regulated entity delivers the relevant financial activity.

[Principles heading]

  1. 01

    Need before product

    We begin with the economic requirement, not with a predetermined financial product.

  2. 02

    Connected, not consolidated

    Businesses, institutions and partners can participate in the ecosystem without necessarily giving up their independence or becoming part of a single corporate structure.

  3. 03

    Evidence before claims

    We distinguish clearly between what exists today, what is being developed and what is proposed, and we support claims with evidence wherever possible.

  4. 04

    Impact built into the architecture of capital

    Impact should not be treated only as an assessment after capital is deployed. It should influence how capital is structured, allocated and managed from the outset.

  5. 05

    Clear governance and accountability

    Ownership, responsibility, regulatory boundaries and decision-making should be transparent and appropriately governed.

  6. 06

    Long-term stewardship and disciplined execution

    We focus on building durable institutions, productive businesses and resilient financial structures rather than pursuing short-term transactions in isolation.
