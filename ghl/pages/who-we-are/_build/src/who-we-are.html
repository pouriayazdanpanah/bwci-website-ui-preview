Why BWCI exists

Mission

Our purpose is to improve how capital reaches productive activity. We start with what a business or project needs, then design financial architecture that matches it with capital, institutions, technology and partners. The aim is productive growth, resilient businesses and long-term impact.

What “Better World” means

“Better World” names a view about where impact belongs. Impact is often assessed after capital has already been allocated. Our approach considers it earlier: when capital is structured, owned and deployed.

Our role, in brief

BWCI is the financial architecture layer of the Better World ecosystem: it designs financial structures, connects participants, coordinates their roles and develops the ecosystem. Any financial activity is delivered by the relevant operating or regulated entity.

Its role is to:

  1. identify and understand underlying economic and financing requirements;
  2. design appropriate financial architecture around those requirements;
  3. connect businesses and projects with relevant sources of capital, institutions and capabilities;
  4. coordinate participation between investors, financial institutions, operating entities and strategic partners;
  5. develop and support platforms and investment vehicles where appropriate;
  6. facilitate the integration of capital, financial capability, technology and strategic relationships; and
  7. support the development of long-term financial infrastructure serving productive economic activity.

Our Principles

Six principles describe how we work and what we expect to be held to.

  1. 01

    Need before product

    We start with the economic requirement of a business or project, not with a predetermined financial product. The structure is then designed around that need.

  2. 02

    Connected, not consolidated

    Businesses, institutions and partners can take part in the Better World ecosystem without giving up their independence. Our Role explains how they connect and what the principle does not cover.

  3. 03

    Evidence before claims

    We distinguish clearly between what is operating today, what is in development and what is proposed. Each claim we make about BWCI and the Better World ecosystem should be supported by evidence.

  4. 04

    Impact built into the architecture of capital

    Impact should shape how capital is allocated and managed from the start. It belongs in the design of a structure, not only in a review afterwards.

  5. 05

    Clear governance and accountability

    Ownership, responsibility, regulatory boundaries and decision-making should be transparent and well governed. Each participant’s role, and who answers for it, should be clear.

  6. 06

    Long-term stewardship and disciplined execution

    We aim to build durable institutions and resilient structures, rather than isolated short-term deals. Each step should be planned and delivered with that long-term purpose in view.

What you can expect from us

  1. We listen first. Every conversation starts with what you need, not with what we want to sell.

  2. We tell you who does what. You will always know which entity is responsible for any regulated activity.

  3. We are honest about where we are. We say clearly what is operating today and what is still being built.

  4. We respect your independence. Working with us does not require you to give up ownership or control.

  5. We only claim what we can evidence, including on impact.
