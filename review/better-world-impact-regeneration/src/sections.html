Regeneration

What we mean by regeneration

A regenerative business restores more than it takes:

  1. 01

    Natural

    natural resources, such as soil, water and materials kept in use;

  2. 02

    Economic

    economic resources, such as local supply chains, skills and jobs;

  3. 03

    Social

    social resources, such as the strength of the communities around it.

We favour capital structures that reward that kind of business: patient enough for long payback periods, shaped around real cash flows, and governed so the intended outcomes stay in view while the capital is committed.

For example: circular manufacturing that recovers and reuses materials, regenerative land use, cleaner energy and the regional suppliers that keep production close to home.

Three returns

Three returns from every cycle

Every cycle is designed to return three things:

  • Financial return

    For the investors whose capital made it possible.

  • Economic return

    For the business and its region, in trade, jobs and resilience.

  • Regenerative return

    For the natural, economic and social resources the business depends on.

In development

How we will measure them

Our Impact and Outcomes methodology is in development. It will set out how we measure all three returns and how we report them, and it will be published here.

Until then, we will not make impact claims we cannot evidence. Case studies will be added as platforms become operational and outcomes can be verified.
