UI design work: Who We Are

A record of the interface design completed on the Who We Are page, the shared system it sits on, and the items still open.

1. Summary

  • Who We Are was built from the wireframe as a nine-section page and then modernised section by section to match the home page and the rest of the site.
  • The page uses the shared header, breadcrumb, footer and bwci-system.css, so type scale, spacing, buttons and motion match across the site.

2. Shared foundation

Area What was set
ColourBrand ink #123039, teal #1D4A54, emerald #12836B, gold #B98F2D; status colours for Operational (green), In development (gold) and Proposed (grey).
TypographyPlus Jakarta Sans for text, JetBrains Mono for labels, dates and table headers. Display 36–60px, section H1 28–42px, body-lg 16–18px. Mobile floors: labels ≥14px, body ≥16px.
SpacingSection padding 64–112px, side padding 20–40px, 1280px container, ~42px between section heading and content.
Buttons and links48px min height, 6px radius, arrow nudges 3px on hover, 0.97 press scale. Text links use an underline that grows from left on hover.
MotionStaggered hero entrance, scroll reveal on sections, all reduced to simple fades under prefers-reduced-motion.
Shared partsSiteHeader (sticky), Breadcrumb, SiteFooter, PersonCard.

3. Page sections

Built from the wireframe, then updated section by section. Eyebrow labels and approval badges were removed across the page so headings lead directly.

4. Consistency and QA

  • Section heading sizes on Who We Are matched to the other pages.
  • Heading-to-content gaps normalised to roughly 42px.
  • Responsive checks at desktop, tablet and mobile, including overflow checks at 320, 360, 375, 390 and 430px widths.
  • Type audit at 390px before and after the mobile type floors were applied.
  • Keyboard focus rings (2px emerald) on links, buttons and form fields; reduced-motion fallbacks on all animation.
  • Automated review run twice on Who We Are; the final fix was the quote card link width.
