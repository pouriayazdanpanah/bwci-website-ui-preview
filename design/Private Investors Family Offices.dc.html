Why private and family capital matters

Private and family capital can take a long view. That patience matters to productive businesses and projects that need time to build, and that capital does not always reach.

Capital is not one thing. Different capital belongs in different parts of the architecture: some suits trade activity, some long-term ownership, some long-duration institution-building. Our work is to connect each kind with the part where it fits.

  • Trade activityShorter cycle
  • Long-term ownershipPatient
  • Long-duration institution-buildingGenerational
