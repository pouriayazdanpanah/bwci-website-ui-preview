UI design work: Corporate & Investor Relations and Who We Are

A record of the interface design completed on the two page families, the shared system they sit on, and the items still open.

1. Summary

  • Corporate & IR was built as one design component covering six sub-pages (Hub, Company, Reports, Share, Regulatory, Legal) with a sticky section nav, responsive data tables, document filters, and a "To confirm" flag system for unapproved content.
  • Who We Are was built from the wireframe as a nine-section page and then modernised section by section to match the home page and the rest of the site.
  • Both pages use the shared header, breadcrumb, footer and bwci-system.css, so type scale, spacing, buttons and motion match across the site.

3.2 Components designed

  • Section nav. White bar that sticks under the site header. Desktop: centred tabs, 56px tall, 3px ink underline on the current tab. Narrow screens: a labelled "Section" dropdown.
  • Data tables. 14px-radius bordered tables with mono uppercase headers, row hover tint, and per-table column grids. On tablet, low-priority columns are hidden; on mobile, rows stack into cards with inline mono labels.
  • Filters. 48px rounded selects and search field, emerald focus ring, "Clear" text button. Four columns on desktop, two on tablet, one on mobile.
  • Announcements list. Date / title / arrow rows; the arrow slides 4px and turns emerald on hover. Date moves above the title on mobile.
  • Verify a communication. Cloud-tinted panel, intro on the left and a numbered rule list (gold mono numerals) on the right. Stacks below 900px.
  • Regulatory perimeter. Dark ink panel with a faint dot pattern fading from the top-right corner, gold label and large statement text.
  • Status badges. Pill badges for Operational and Proposed entities, plus inline gold flag notes.
  • Group tree. Parent card with child entities connected by hairline branches.
  • "To confirm" flags. Gold pills beside any heading whose content awaits approval, switchable on and off. Group structure stays hidden until approved.

5. Consistency and QA

  • Section heading sizes on Who We Are matched to the other pages.
  • Heading-to-content gaps normalised to roughly 42px.
  • Responsive checks at desktop, tablet and mobile, including overflow checks at 320, 360, 375, 390 and 430px widths.
  • Type audit at 390px before and after the mobile type floors were applied.
  • Keyboard focus rings (2px emerald) on links, buttons and form fields; reduced-motion fallbacks on all animation.
  • Automated review run twice on Who We Are; the final fix was the quote card link width.
